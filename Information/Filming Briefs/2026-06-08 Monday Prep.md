### Hook and Script Architecture

**Hook type:** Outcome — lead with the result the viewer wants, not with who Luke is.

**Opening line (first 3 seconds):**
"This one mistake is costing you 20 yards off the tee."

**Beat structure:**

| Beat | Timing | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3s | Opening line above. Luke direct to camera, no intro, no "Hi I'm Luke". |
| Problem | 3 to 8s | Name the pain in the viewer's words. "Most golfers know something's off but don't know what." |
| Solution | 8 to 30s | Luke demonstrates or explains the fix. 2 to 4 second cuts, alternate wide and close. No same shot held past 3 sentences. |
| CTA | Last 5s | Value exchange only. "Get your free assessment at the link in our bio and find out what's holding your game back." |

**Reference video:** Luke's own coaching content on @lukenorthwoodgolf. Match his natural delivery pace, not a polished ad pace.

---

### Shotlist (9:16 master)

| Shot | Frame | Description | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium, eye level | Luke direct to camera, opening line | Hook |
| 2 | Close, hands | Club grip or setup position | Problem beat detail |
| 3 | Wide | Luke addressing the ball, full swing | Solution wide |
| 4 | Close | Simulator screen showing data (ball speed, launch angle, spin) | Solution detail, tech credibility |
| 5 | Medium | Luke reacting to the data, pointing at screen | Solution explanation |
| 6 | Close | Hands adjusting grip or stance on Luke's direction | Solution teaching moment |
| 7 | Wide | Before and after swing comparison if time allows | Solution payoff |
| 8 | Medium | Luke direct to camera, CTA | CTA |

---

### Sequence (filming order, not edit order)

Group by setup to avoid moving equipment mid-shoot.

1. Camera at simulator bay, Luke at the ball. Shoot all swing and demo shots first (Shots 3, 4, 6, 7) while energy is high and lighting is consistent.
2. Move to medium setup. Shoot Luke at screen, reacting and explaining (Shot 5).
3. Keep medium setup. Shoot opening line to camera (Shot 1) and CTA (Shot 8).
4. Get close cutaway shots last — hands, grip, club detail (Shot 2).

**Cut cadence:** every 2 to 4 seconds across the solution beat. Do not hold on Luke talking for more than 3 sentences without a cut to a new frame.
