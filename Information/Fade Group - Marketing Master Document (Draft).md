## PART 1 — GROUP CONTEXT

---

### Why Fade Group Exists

Fade Group is a Leamington Spa and Warwick-based group operating across golf, podiatry, and wellness. The business exists to give people access to premium, expert-led services in sport, health, and performance — all in one place in Warwickshire.

The group is built around three complementary brands: a golf brand centred on improvement and aspiration, a podiatry brand centred on health outcomes and expert care, and a wellness brand centred on recovery, movement, and community. Together they serve overlapping audiences at different stages of the same lifestyle — active, health-conscious people who want to perform, recover, and feel better.

---

### The Three Brands

| Brand | What It Does | Core Proposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Fade Golf** | Golf simulator, memberships, coaching, assessments | Premium coaching and performance improvement led by expert instructors. Luke Northwood (PGA Pro) is the face of the brand. |
| **Stride Clinics** | Podiatry, biomechanics, orthotics, sports massage | Expert clinical care for foot health, movement, and injury. Where you go when you want the cause fixed, not just the symptom. |
| **Fire & Earth Leamington** | Physiotherapy, S&C, sports massage, run club, wellness coworking | Premium wellness and performance hub. Recovery, conditioning, and community in one place. |

The three brands share a group identity — professional, expert, locally rooted — but each has its own distinct voice, audience, and visual identity. Marketing should always feel brand-specific, not group-generic.

# FADE GOLF

---

### Identity

**Brand positioning**
Fade Golf is a premium golf brand built around expert coaching, simulator technology, and membership. The strategic centre is Luke Northwood — PGA Pro and face of the brand — whose swing and expertise give Fade Golf credibility that no amount of generic marketing can replicate. The funnel runs from awareness through free assessments into memberships. Content should feel expert and aspirational: *we know what we are talking about, and that is why you need to learn from Luke/us.*

**Brand voice**
- **Tone:** Trustworthy, expert, premium, warm and welcoming, fun, aspirational but accessible, confident without arrogance. Not stuffy, not trying-too-hard, not laddy.
- **Use:** Pro level · Dream swing · Get assessed · Find out what's holding you back · Best version of your game · Our coaches · Elite · Part of the Fade family · Tournament ready · Members / Golfers · Sessions / Coaching
- **Avoid:** Revolutionary · Lads · Smash it · Banter · Crushing it · Clients · Packages · Facility
- **Rhythm:** Premium pacing. No exclamation pile-ons. No "Hey guys" openers.
- **Good reference:** [Real journey post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DVbwOwojj7r/) · [Pain/injury angle](https://www.instagram.com/p/DUI4lzaiNN_/)
- **Drift catcher:** [Long-form pro vs amateur format](https://www.instagram.com/p/DUlqqYtjnp5/) — concept can appeal but people won't invest the time. Format doesn't earn its length.

**Visual identity**
- **Primary colours:** `#41873c` (green), `#000000` (black)
- **Secondary:** `#ffffff` (white)
- **Logo rule:** always on a black background
- **Fonts:** Anton (headlines), Glacial Indifference (body)
- **Photography:** premium golf photography. Real over stock. Movement and skill in frame, not posed social shots. Fun but premium — not laddy.
- **Avoid:** Red (F&E), blue (Stride), gold. Different shades of green in the same grid. Different headline fonts across content.
- **Good design reference:** [Clean, informative post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DVeTWjziLEe/)
- **Drift catcher:** [Off-brand font/colour](https://www.instagram.com/p/DJLh89Rt5wd/?img_index=1)

---

### Audience

**Primary:** Men aged 25–60, local to Leamington and Warwick. Professional-class (JLR, Aston Martin, business owners). Have the time and budget to work on their swing seriously. Some injured or looking to extend their prime golf years.

**Secondary:** Younger men, partly playing for ego or newer to golf and unsure of their technique.

**Triggers:**
- "How can I get better at this?"
- "I don't want to be the worst amongst my mates"
- "Where can I get a professional opinion?"

**Barriers:**
- Don't know they're doing something wrong
- Assume it costs too much
- Pride — reluctant to admit they need help
- Older audience not informed about simulator technology

**Where they are online:** Instagram and Facebook. Follow golf accounts and pro golfers.

**Where they are offline:** Higher-end bars and gastro pubs. Pubs near golf courses and driving ranges.

**How they find us:** "Golf range near me" · "Golf sim near me" · "Golfing lessons in Warwick" · "Golfers in Leamington"

---

### Strategy

**Content pillars**
1. Luke — coaching, expertise, personality
2. Simulator — the experience, data, results
3. Member stories — real journeys, real improvement
4. Free assessment — recurring offer, Luke-led
5. Behind the scenes — Jason, the team, the facilities

**Channel strategy**

| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram (@fadegolf.uk) | Primary. Reels and short video with scroll-stopping covers. Static posts not performing. | Regular — rebuild cadence with Luke content |
| Facebook (Fade Golf UK) | Secondary, mirrors Instagram. Same performance pattern. | Consistent with Instagram |
| YouTube (@FadeGolfUK) | One post (podcast with Luke). Potential for longer-form content if format pivots. | TBC |
| TikTok | Not active. If short-form video becomes the primary route, TikTok is the right platform for cross-posting. | TBC |

**Customer journey**
1. *Discovery:* Instagram Reel or Facebook post — Luke's swing, a challenge video, member story
2. *Interest:* Free assessment offer — low commitment, Luke delivers it
3. *Conversion:* Assessment → membership pitch. Luke built into the membership offer explicitly
4. *Retention:* Ongoing coaching, simulator sessions, community feel, ambassador content

**Active campaigns:** Free assessment giveaway (draw 25 June, shortening under review) · Luke ambassador content (filming dates TBC) · Father & Son giveaway (before 15 June) · Pro-am tie-in (pending Luke's pricing confirmation)

# STRIDE CLINICS

---

### Identity

**Brand positioning**
Stride is a clinical podiatry brand delivering expert podiatry, biomechanics, and orthotics. The brand sits in the premium end of private podiatry — credible, warm, and service-specific. Content leads with the patient problem and follows with the clinical answer. The promise is treating the cause, not just the symptom.

**Brand voice**
- **Tone:** Trustworthy, warm, expert, clinical, professional. Plain English for non-medical readers. Service shading: podiatry/nail surgery (reassuring, older demographic), biomechanics/gait analysis (performance-aware, active adults), orthotics (medical-credible, performance-aware).
- **Use:** Professional nail treatment · Treating the cause, not just the symptoms · Getting you moving again · Private podiatry · Expert foot care · Back on your feet · Pain that won't go away · Mobility · Nail surgery
- **Avoid:** Budget · Free consultation · Simple procedure · Basic treatment · Cut price · Guaranteed results · "Just an ingrown toenail" · Medical jargon for non-medical readers
- **Rhythm:** Plain, clinical, reassuring. No exclamation pile-ons. No casual openers. Lead with the patient problem, follow with the clinical answer.
- **Good reference:** [360 Podiatry](https://www.instagram.com/360podiatry/) · [Foot scan cyclists post](https://www.instagram.com/p/Ci0gDYhDv1z/) · [Footscan Centre Newcastle](https://www.instagram.com/footscan_centre_newcastle/)
- **Drift catchers:** [Off-voice post 1](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFLg4-zqQ--/) · [Off-voice post 2](https://www.instagram.com/p/DOEaiXbDBnH/?img_index=1)

**Visual identity**
- **Primary colours:** `#204f7f` (Stride blue), `#000000` (black)
- **Secondary:** `#ffffff` (white)
- **Logo rule:** always on a white background
- **Fonts:** Raleway (Titles), Glacial Indifference (Subheadings). Adjust caps and bold for subtext and titles.
- **Photography:** Clinical, professional, expert. Real photos of clinic, team, or patients (with consent). Soft natural light. 3/4 angle on people, warm light not cold.
- **Avoid:** Green (Fade Golf), red (F&E — reads as medical/emergency). Harsh clinical-cold blue. Stock illustrations of feet. Anonymous medical team-in-scrubs stock. Anything gross or "feet video" style.
- **Compliance:** Patient photos and before/afters require written consent. Medical claims need Harry sign-off. No "cure", "permanent fix", or "guaranteed" language.

---

### Audience

**Primary:** Adults aged 30–85, Leamington and Warwick. Mixed gender. People with foot pain, nail conditions, or movement problems who want expert private care rather than waiting for NHS treatment.

**Secondary:** Active adults, amateur athletes and children (10–45) who want biomechanics assessments, custom orthotics or treatments.

**Triggers:**
- "This toe isn't getting better on its own"
- "I'm waking up in pain every day"
- "I don't want to be told to stop training"
- "I want to get back to where I was before"

**Barriers:**
- Not knowing if the problem is serious enough to book
- Assuming over the counter treatment is sufficient
- Pride (older demographic) — reluctance to seek help for something "minor"

**Where they are online:** Instagram and Facebook. Local Leamington community groups on Facebook are significant for the 35–50 age group.

**Where they are offline:** Local gyms, running clubs, GP waiting rooms, church groups, community spots, cafes.

**How they find us:** "Private podiatry Leamington" · "Quick verruca treatment" · "Ingrown toenail relief" · "Foot pain that won't go away"

---

### Strategy

**Content pillars**
1. Service education — what each treatment is, who it's for, what to expect
2. Patient outcomes — before/afters, success stories (consent-first)
3. Expert insight — tips, myth-busting, things to know
4. Behind the scenes — team, clinic, equipment
5. Biomechanics and orthotics — gait analysis, foot health awareness

**Channel strategy**

| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram (@strideclinics) | Primary. Informative posts and reels perform well. | Regular — rebuild cadence with new photos from 1 June shoot |
| Facebook | Secondary, mirrors Instagram. Adapt for slightly older demographic. | Consistent with Instagram |
| Google Ads | Per-service campaigns — restructure pending Google tag fix | Once tag is live |
| Email | No list currently. Strong opportunity for older demographic newsletter. | TBC |

**Customer journey**
1. *Discovery:* Instagram post or Facebook community group — symptom-led content ("pain that won't go away")
2. *Interest:* Educational content builds trust — service explanation, team credibility
3. *Conversion:* Direct booking via website or phone
4. *Retention:* Follow-up content, orthotics relaunch July, repeat booking for ongoing podiatry care

**Active campaigns:** Waiting area ads (5 treatments — in progress) · Orthotics relaunch July · Google Ads restructure (once tag live) · Website refresh (new photos from 1 June shoot)

---

### Performance

**What's working:** Informative posts ("5 signs you need X" style). Content with real team members in frame. Service-specific posts that speak to a named problem.

**What's not working:** Ad-focused posts. Generic promotional content without a clear patient problem.

**Seasonal patterns:** TBC — not enough Metricool data yet. Running-related content likely peaks spring/summer. Nail surgery content may peak in summer (sandal season).

# FIRE & EARTH LEAMINGTON

---

### Identity

**Brand positioning**
Fire & Earth Leamington is a premium wellness and performance hub — physiotherapy, strength and conditioning, sports massage, run club, and wellness coworking in one space. The brand is in an active rebrand toward a more elevated, premium positioning. It is not athlete-only and not a chain-wellness spa. The positioning is: *you don't have to be an athlete to feel better in your body. Whether you're in pain, recovering, or just ready to feel like yourself again — F&E is here.*

**Brand voice**
- **Tone:** Premium, considered, grounded, accessible. Not over-excited, not laddy, not clinical-cold.
- **Use:** Recover properly · Feel like yourself again · Relief · In good hands · At your own pace · Highly qualified therapists · Back to yourself · Pain free · Move better
- **Avoid:** Bespoke · Smash your goals · Book now to avoid disappointment · Life-changing · Sports massage (rebrand to "deep tissue in motion") · For athletes · For runners · Quick fix · Generic wellness platitudes unless used pointedly
- **Rhythm:** Considered, not breathless. No double exclamation marks. No "Hey guys". No all-caps. No emoji clusters. Read like a credible sports therapy clinic.
- **Good reference:** [Informative, problem-led post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DOodbDhArzc/?img_index=1) · [Misconception-clearing post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQxCVQjDV7_/)
- **Drift catchers:** *"Ready to SMASH your recovery goals? 💪"* — aggressive, all-caps, emoji-heavy, budget-gym feel. *"Hey guys! 👋 So we've got some AMAZING news…"* — over-excited, unprofessional, undermines premium positioning.

**Visual identity**
- **Primary colour:** `#b01227` (F&E red)
- **Secondary:** `#181206` (near-black), `#ffffff` (white)
- **Accent:** `#bfb196` (warm neutral, sparingly)
- **Logo rule:** light or dark background depending on which logo variant is used. Correct logo to be confirmed with Harry (rebrand in progress as of June 2026)
- **Fonts:** See Canva and existing leaflets, or check with Olivia (F&E design touchpoint)
- **Photography:** Real clinic photos. Friendly team photos. Action shots of services. Premium feel that earns trust — show that therapists are experienced and can get people out of pain.
- **Avoid:** Green (Fade Golf), blue (Stride). Generic spa-pastels. Stock wellness imagery (woman on yoga mat, detached candle). Raw event shots without a proper round-up write-up.

---

### Audience

**Primary:** Adults aged 25–60, mixed gender, Leamington/Warwick/Kenilworth. Professionally doing well but not wealthy — teachers, nurses, office managers, small business owners. Gym members, hobby athletes, parkrunners. Often juggling work and family, with their own health coming last.

**Secondary 1:** Performance-focused amateur athlete (25–40, male). Gym-regular, plays football/rugby/golf at weekends. Wants S&C and sports massage for maintenance, not because something is broken. Proactive. Likely crossover with Fade Golf.

**Secondary 2:** Post-injury returner (30–50, mixed gender). Significant injury or long-term absence from sport. Has been through NHS physio and wants the next stage.

**Triggers:**
- "This knee isn't getting better on its own"
- "I've got that 10k in 8 weeks, I can't keep ignoring it"
- "I keep getting the same injury"
- "I want to get back to where I was before, not just functional"

**Barriers:**
- "What if they tell me something I don't want to hear"
- "I don't know if I need a physio or a sports therapist or a massage"
- "I don't want to be told to stop training"

**Where they are online:** Instagram (primary, scrolling evenings). Facebook local community groups (Leamington Spa Noticeboard — significant for 35–50 age group).

**Where they are offline:** Local gyms, yoga and Pilates studios, swimming clubs, independent coffee shops, parkrun.

**How they find us:** "Physiotherapists in Leamington Spa" · "Sports massage Leamington" · "Strength and conditioning Leamington" · "Running injury physio near me" · "Knee pain won't go away"

**Customer journey**

4. *Retention:* Run Club ongoing, treatment follow-up, events and partnerships
