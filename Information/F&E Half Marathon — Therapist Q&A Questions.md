**7 June 2026**

## Therapist to Camera

1. What's the most common injury you see at events like this, and how do you treat it on the day?
2. What's the difference between something you can run through and something that needs immediate attention?
3. What should runners do in the first 24 hours after a race to help their body recover?

## Therapist Talking to Customers

1. Where's the pain — is it sharp or more of a dull ache?
2. How long have you been feeling this — did it come on during the race or before?
3. Is this something that's come up before, or is this new for you?

## Chat with Katie — Run Club

1. So Katie, tell us — what is Stride Run Club and when does it kick off?
2. Who's it for — do you need to be a serious runner to get involved?
3. What made you want to get involved with it?
4. What can people expect when they come along for the first time?
5. If someone's watching this and thinking about joining — what would you say to them?

## Post-race Chat with Runners

1. So how did you get on today — was it everything you expected?
2. What made you sign up for this one?
3. Is this your first time at an event like this, or are you a regular?
4. What's kept you going through the training?
5. Would you come back next year?
