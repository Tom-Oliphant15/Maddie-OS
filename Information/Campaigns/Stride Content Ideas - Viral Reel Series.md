## Overview

5 high-share-potential content ideas tailored to Stride's social media direction. These are a series — work through them over time, not all at once. When booking a shoot, refer to the "Footage needed" section for each so nothing gets missed on the day.

## Idea 1 — "I've had this for years and didn't know it could be fixed"

**Format:** 30-second talking head Reel — Helen or Wendy to camera
**Hook:** *"If you've had [ingrown toenail / heel pain / verruca] for more than 3 months, here's what's actually happening..."*
**Why it works:** Speaks directly to people who've normalised their pain. Massive share and tag potential — people send it to friends and family.
**Best service angle:** Nail surgery or Verrutop
**CTA:** Soft — "link in bio to book"

**Footage needed for shoot:**
- Helen or Wendy to camera, clean background, good light
- Confident, warm delivery — not scripted-sounding
- Film 2-3 takes with different service angles (ingrown toenail / heel pain / verruca) so you have options

---

## Idea 2 — Gait Plate Reveal — "This is why your knee hurts"

**Format:** 15-second silent Reel with text overlay
**Hook:** *"This is what your feet are actually doing when you walk."*
**Structure:** Lynda walking across gait plate → cut to Wendy pointing at pressure reading on screen → text overlay "Did you know this could be causing your knee/hip/back pain?"
**Why it works:** The reveal format is one of the highest-performing Reel structures. People watch twice and share. No words needed — the visual does everything.
**Best service angle:** Biomechanics / Gait Analysis

**Footage needed for shoot (Wendy top-up session):**
- Lynda (or any model) walking across the gait plate — slow, clear, side angle
- Close-up of the pressure plate screen showing the foot scan result
- Wendy pointing at the screen, explaining (even silently)
- Film in sequence so the reveal edit is easy

## Idea 3 — "Things podiatrists wish people would stop doing" Series

**Format:** 20-30 second talking head Reels — one per episode, recurring series
**Hook example:** *"Cutting down the side of an ingrown toenail at home. Stop. Here's why."*
**Why it works:** High comment bait — people relate, share, or debate. Series format builds followers faster than one-off posts. Do 5 back to back on different days.

**Episode ideas:**
1. Cutting down the side of an ingrown toenail at home
2. Ignoring heel pain and hoping it goes away
3. Buying insoles from Boots instead of getting a proper assessment
4. Leaving a verruca untreated for years
5. Wearing shoes that are too small (and what it does to your toes over time)

**Footage needed for shoot:**
- Helen or Wendy to camera, same setup each time for series consistency
- Film all 5 in one session to batch the content
- Relaxed, direct tone — not preachy

## Idea 4 — Before/After (Clinical, Not Gross)

**Format:** Still image carousel or short Reel — outcome focused, not procedure focused
**Hook:** *"6 weeks ago this patient couldn't walk without pain."*
**Why it works:** Before/after is the most shared podiatry content. Stride's angle is clean and outcome-led — show the result, not the graphic procedure.
**Important:** Consent-first. Patient must sign a release before any identifiable content is used. Even a well-framed still image carousel works — doesn't have to be video.

**Footage needed for shoot:**
- "After" shots — clean heels, resolved nail, patient walking comfortably
- Needs a matching "before" captured at the initial appointment
- Build a habit of photographing at first appointment (with consent) so the before/after pipeline grows over time

**Action:** Raise with Helen + Wendy — start capturing before shots at appointments with a consent form. Tom to advise on consent wording if needed.

## Idea 5 — "Rate Your Foot Health" Interactive Carousel

**Format:** 5-slide Canva carousel
**Hook:** *"Rate your foot health — swipe to find out."*
**Structure:**
- Slide 1: Hook + "swipe to score yourself"
- Slides 2-4: Symptoms with a point score (ingrown nail = 2pts, heel pain in the morning = 3pts, verruca = 1pt, aching feet after a day on your feet = 2pts)
- Slide 5: "If you scored over 5, it's time to book. Link in bio."
**Why it works:** High save rate = algorithm boost. Instagram pushes carousels hard right now. Easy to make in Canva — no new footage needed.
