# Fire & Earth Leamington — Viral Instagram Content Plan

*Created: 2026-06-02. Status: Ready to execute when filming days are scheduled.*

---

## The 5 Ideas

Based on research into the top 10 viral Instagram trends in physiotherapy, strength & conditioning, and run club content (June 2026). All five tailored to F&E's three content pillars: physio, S&C, and Run Club.

---

### 1. "I Don't Even Know If I Need A Physio" — Myth Buster Series
**Format:** 20–30 sec talking head Reel — Olivia or therapist to camera
**Hook:** *"You don't need to be in agony to come and see us. Here's who we actually see every week."*
**Structure:** Therapist lists 3 real types of people who walk through the door — the person whose knee has been niggling for months, the runner who keeps getting the same injury, the person who doesn't want to be told to stop training. One sentence each. End: *"If you've been putting it off, that's exactly who we're here for."*
**Why it works:** F&E's own audience data says the #1 barrier to booking is not knowing if they need physio and fear of being told bad news. This directly dismantles that. Myth-busting physio reels are consistently high-save and high-share — people send them to friends who need to hear it.
**CTA:** "First appointment — link in bio."

**Footage needed:**
- Olivia or therapist to camera, clean background, good light
- Warm, direct delivery — not scripted-sounding
- Film 2–3 takes with slightly different angles (knee pain / recurring injury / "don't want to stop training") so you have options

---

### 2. "Same Injury, Third Time" — The Root Cause Reveal
**Format:** 15–20 sec Reel — split screen or single angle with text overlay
**Hook:** *"If you keep getting the same injury, it's not bad luck. Here's what's actually going on."*
**Structure:** Therapist explains in 2 sentences that recurring injuries are almost always a movement pattern or weakness issue, not the injury itself. Cut to a simple demonstration — mobility test, single-leg squat, or gait clip. Text overlay: *"Stop treating the symptom. Fix the cause."*
**Why it works:** "I keep getting the same injury, there must be a reason" is one of F&E's exact audience trigger phrases. The reveal format drives saves and shares — people screenshot and send to training partners.
**CTA:** "Book an assessment. Link in bio."

**Footage needed:**
- Therapist to camera — 2 sentences max
- Simple movement demo: single-leg squat, hip hinge, or mobility test
- Clean floor space, decent light — no equipment needed

---

### 3. Run Club "First Timer" Documentary
**Format:** 30–45 sec Reel — vlog-style, handheld, natural
**Hook:** *"She'd never run more than 5 minutes before tonight."*
**Structure:** Follow one first-timer through a single Run Club session. Pre-run nerves to camera → group setting off → mid-run check-in → the finish → their face at the end. Raw is the format. Caption: *"This is what Tuesday nights look like at F&E Run Club. Everyone starts somewhere."*
**Why it works:** Run club community content is one of the biggest social fitness movements of 2026 — community-first, not performance-first. First-timer content outperforms polished promotional content every time. The tag-a-friend mechanic is built in.
**Series potential:** Recurring monthly feature — new face, new story, same warmth.

**Footage needed:**
- One willing first-timer (ask Katie in advance)
- Pre-run reaction to camera (10 seconds)
- Group shot at the start
- Mid-run clip — doesn't need to be polished
- Finish line / end of run reaction
- Shoot handheld on phone throughout — natural light, no setup needed

---

### 4. S&C "One Thing That Changed Everything" — 30-Second Fix
**Format:** 15–20 sec Reel — exercise demo, clean and direct
**Hook:** *"If you do one thing differently in your training this week, make it this."*
**Structure:** Therapist demonstrates one corrective exercise or mobility drill simple enough to do at home. 15 seconds of the exercise, 5 seconds of why it matters. Text overlay keeps it clean.

**Episode ideas (batch film all in one session):**
1. Hip flexor release — for runners and desk workers
2. Single-leg balance — ankle stability and injury prevention
3. Thoracic rotation — for anyone who sits at a desk all day
4. Glute activation — for runners with knee or hip pain
5. Calf raise progression — Achilles and ankle load management

**Why it works:** Saves are Instagram's strongest ranking signal in 2026. Educational content people want to return to drives saves more than anything else. One per week = always-on series that builds follower growth consistently.

**Footage needed:**
- Any therapist, clean background, 10 minutes per episode
- Film all 5 in one session to batch content
- No patient needed, no equipment needed

---

### 5. "Can You Pass The Test?" — Interactive Movement Screen
**Format:** 20–30 sec Reel
**Hook:** *"This is the simple test we use to check if your hips are the reason you keep getting injured. Try it now."*
**Structure:** Therapist demonstrates a functional movement screen — single-leg squat, overhead squat, or standing hip hinge — and gives viewers a real-time pass/fail. *"If you wobble here, that's your answer."* End CTA: *"Tell us how you got on in the comments."*
**Why it works:** Interactive content that makes the audience do something drives comments — the second strongest ranking signal after saves. If someone fails the test, they book. The comment section becomes social proof.

**Footage needed:**
- One therapist, one camera, clear floor space
- Demonstrate the movement slowly and clearly
- Film a pass version and a fail version so the edit shows both
- No equipment needed
