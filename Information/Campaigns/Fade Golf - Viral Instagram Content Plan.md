# Fade Golf — Viral Instagram Content Plan

*Created: 2026-06-02. Status: Ready to execute when filming dates are confirmed with Luke/Jason/Harry.*

---

## The 5 Ideas

Based on research into the top 10 viral golf Instagram trends in June 2026. All five are tailored to Fade Golf's simulator, Luke as face-of-brand, and the target audience (men 25–60, local, professional-class, ego + improvement motivated).

---

### 1. "First Swing" Challenge
**Format:** 15–30 sec Reel
**Concept:** Film a complete beginner hitting their very first shot on the simulator. Zero coaching. Raw reaction. Caption: *"We put a first-timer on our simulator. This happened."* Tag them. Ask followers to tag someone who needs to try it.
**Why it works:** Wyatt Kelce's debut swing went global in April 2026 — same format. The tag-a-friend mechanic is built in. Simulator lowers the intimidation barrier.
**Paid CTA hook:** "Free assessment — link in bio."

---

### 2. Luke's Brutal Honest Swing Review
**Format:** 20–40 sec Reel — face-to-camera + slow-mo split screen
**Concept:** Luke watches a real member's swing (submitted by DM) and gives a completely honest one-sentence verdict to camera. Split screen: their swing on one side, Luke's reaction on the other. Caption: *"He asked for feedback. Luke didn't hold back."*
**Why it works:** User-generated content + a face people want to impress = comments, saves, shares. Series potential — run weekly with #SwingReview.
**Execution:** DM callout 3–4 days before Luke Day. "Send us your swing. Luke will review it."

---

### 3. "Can You Beat The Simulator?" Closest-to-the-Pin Challenge
**Format:** 30 sec Reel
**Concept:** Closest-to-the-pin competition on the simulator. Film 5–10 people back to back — members, staff, Luke. Leaderboard on screen. Final shot wins a free round or free assessment.
**Why it works:** Challenge + leaderboard + tension + payoff = the dopamine loop that drives shares. Every shot is measurable — no arguing. Can become a recurring monthly competition.

---

### 4. Before/After: 4-Week Swing Transformation
**Format:** 60 sec Reel (built from 4 x weekly 15-sec clips leading to the payoff)
**Concept:** One real member. Week 1: raw first swing. Weeks 2–3: training clips. Week 4: reveal. Side-by-side slow-mo with simulator data.
**Why it works:** Transformation content is the highest-save format on Instagram. The simulator data (ball speed, launch angle) makes the improvement visual and undeniable.
**Paid CTA:** "Book your free assessment. This could be you."

---

### 5. "What's Your Handicap?" — The Handicap Files
**Format:** 15–20 sec Reel
**Concept:** Ask someone their handicap before they hit. They say something confident. Simulator shows the data. Cut to their face. No commentary. Caption: *"He said 12. The data said otherwise."*
**Why it works:** Relatable humour + self-deprecation = highest share rate in golf content. The simulator gives it the credibility. Endlessly repeatable.
**Series name:** The Handicap Files.

## Filming Day Structure

### Filming Day 1 — No Luke Needed
**When:** Any day members are in (can happen before Luke is back)
**Duration:** 1.5–2 hours
**Who:** Maddie + 4–6 willing members + one beginner

**Shoot list:**
1. **Handicap Files x3** — ask 3 members before they hit. Film declaration, swing, data, reaction. ~30 minutes.
2. **First Swing x1** — bring a non-golfer. Completely raw, no coaching.
3. **Transformation baseline** — identify one willing member, film their Week 1 swing. Starts the 4-week clock.

**Prep:**
- Book the simulator bay for 2 hours
- Tell members in advance it's a content day — ask who's happy on camera
- Simulator data screen clearly visible in every shot
- Shoot vertically (9:16) on phone

---

### Filming Day 2 — Luke Day (Anchor Shoot)
**When:** Once Luke is back from his wedding — propose dates when contacting Jason/Harry/Luke
**Duration:** 2–3 hours (can combine with the existing swing/membership reels shoot)
**Who:** Luke + Maddie + 3–4 members + Jason (optional)

**Shoot list:**
1. **Closest-to-Pin Challenge** — Luke vs members vs Jason. Simulator picks the winner.
2. **Luke's Swing Review x2** — Luke reacts to 2 pre-submitted member swings.
3. **Transformation Week 2 check-in** — if the 4-week subject can attend, film Luke giving one tip.
4. **Challenge teaser** — 10-sec clip at start of day: "We're running a challenge. Can you beat Luke? Details dropping this week."

**Prep:**
- DM callout for swing submissions goes out 3–4 days before Luke Day
- Tell members about the Closest-to-Pin challenge in advance — 4–6 competitors minimum
- Agree a prize with Harry (free session or free assessment)
- Confirm Luke is comfortable with the honest-reaction format
